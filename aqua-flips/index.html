Aqua Flips - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Aqua Flips (package com.TheaGames.FlipMatch), published by Thea Games, a product of Thea IT Solutions ("we", "us"), handles information. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, settings such as sound, music and vibration, and the card backs you have unlocked) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. If you uninstall the game, this data is removed.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Aqua Flips shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an interstitial ad between some levels, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to earn extra time, a hint, or a new card back.

To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This helps serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this personal data. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the game shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time where the form offers that option. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Children

Aqua Flips is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone, other than the advertising service described above, which receives data directly from your device.

5. Security and retention

Because the game keeps your progress only on your device, we hold no personal data about you to secure or retain.

6. Your choices

  • Skip any rewarded ad; the game is fully playable without watching them.
  • Manage ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete all locally stored data.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
