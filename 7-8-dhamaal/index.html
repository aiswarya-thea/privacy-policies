7-8 Dhamaal - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android card game 7-8 Dhamaal (package com.theagames.seveneightdhamaal), published by Thea Games, a product of Thea IT Solutions ("we", "us"), handles information. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, location or contacts.

The game asks for a player name (or gives you a random one such as "Player_1234"). Please do not use your real full name. The name is stored on your device, and it is shown to the other players in the same room when you play online.

Your game progress and settings (sound, music, vibration, selected and unlocked card backs, series results) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. If you uninstall the game, this data is removed.

2. Online multiplayer (Photon)

Online matches use Photon (Exit Games) servers. To connect you to a room, Photon processes technical data such as your IP address, device information and the player name and game actions you send while playing. This data is used only to run the match. See the Photon privacy policy. Playing against the AI works offline and does not use these servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

7-8 Dhamaal shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an interstitial ad between some games, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to unlock extras such as a new card back.

To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This helps serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this personal data. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the game shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Children

7-8 Dhamaal is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone other than the services described above (Photon for online play and Google AdMob for ads), which receive data directly from your device.

6. Security and retention

Because the game keeps your progress only on your device, we hold no personal data about you to secure or retain. Photon removes room data when the match ends.

7. Your choices

  • Choose any player name you like; avoid using personal details.
  • Skip any rewarded ad; the game is fully playable without watching them.
  • Play against the AI to avoid the online service entirely.
  • Manage ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the game to delete all locally stored data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
